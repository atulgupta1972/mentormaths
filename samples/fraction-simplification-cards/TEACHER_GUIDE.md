# Fraction Simplification Cards (Class 6+)

Figures show **notebook-style cutting** (diagonal strike-through) on cancelled numbers.

**Golden rule:** Cancel only a common **factor** of the *whole* numerator and denominator.

---

## Card 1. Cancel trap — numbers in a sum (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(4+5)/4`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel both 4s → left with 5 (illegal).
- **Why:** The bar means the whole numerator is divided by 4. So (4+5)/4 = 4/4 + 5/4 = 9/4, not 5.
- **Result:** `9/4`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 2. Matching addend trap (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(44+11)/11`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel the two 11s → 44+1 = 45 (wrong).
- **Why:** 11 divides the whole sum. Correct: 55/11 = 5, or 44/11 + 11/11 = 5. Never 45.
- **Result:** `5`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 3. Same idea with subtraction (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(15-5)/5`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel both 5s → 14 or 1 (both wrong).
- **Why:** (15−5)/5 = 10/5 = 2, or 15/5 − 5/5 = 2.
- **Result:** `2`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 4. Cut must hit every term (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(12+8)/4`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel only 12 with 4 → 11 (wrong).
- **Why:** 4 must divide both terms: 12/4 + 8/4 = 5, or 20/4 = 5.
- **Result:** `5`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 5. Three terms (Class 6–7)

- **Hook:** `(6+9+3)/3`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel the last 3 → 15 (wrong).
- **Why:** Every term is divided by 3: 2+3+1 = 6, or 18/3 = 6.
- **Result:** `6`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 6. When cutting is allowed (Class 6–7)

- **Hook:** `2(7+1)/2 vs (2+7)/2`
- **Wrong cancel:** Illegal cancel in a sum vs legal cancel of a factor.
- **Why:** Cancel only a common FACTOR of the whole top. 2(7+1)/2 → 8 is legal. (2+7)/2 cannot cancel the 2.
- **Result:** `8 vs 9/2`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (1), (3) and (4) are correct; (2) is wrong`

## Card 7. Algebra — split (Class 7–8)

- **Hook:** `(4x+2)/2`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel only the +2 → 4x+1 (wrong).
- **Why:** Split: 4x/2 + 2/2 = 2x+1. Or factor: 2(2x+1)/2 = 2x+1.
- **Result:** `2x+1`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 8. Algebra — do not cut x in a sum (Class 7–8)

- **Hook:** `(4x+2)/x`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel x in 4x → claim 6 (classic trap).
- **Why:** Correct: 4 + 2/x. Check x=2: left is 5, so 6 fails.
- **Result:** `4 + 2/x`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (4) are correct; (1) and (3) are wrong`

## Card 9. Algebra — subtract then factor (Class 7–8)

- **Hook:** `(6x-3)/3`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cancel only −3 → 6x−1 (wrong).
- **Why:** Split: 2x−1. Or factor: 3(2x−1)/3 = 2x−1.
- **Result:** `2x-1`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 10. Master — factor then cut (Class 8+)

- **Hook:** `(2x+2)/(x+1)`
- **Wrong cancel:** Cut x with x → claim 2+2 = 4 (classic trap).
- **Why:** Factor: 2(x+1)/(x+1) = 2 (x≠−1). Cancel the common factor (x+1), not the letter x inside a sum.
- **Result:** `2`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are correct; (1) and (4) are wrong`
