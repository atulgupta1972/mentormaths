# Fraction Simplification Cards (Class 6+)

Figures show **notebook-style cutting** (diagonal strike-through) on cancelled numbers.

**Golden rule:** Cancel only a common **factor** of the *whole* numerator and denominator.

---

## Card 1. Cancel trap — numbers in a sum (Class 6+)

- **Teach figure:** `(4+5)/4`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(6+9)/6`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel both 4s → left with 5 (illegal).
- **Why (quiz):** Same rule as the card: the bar divides the whole sum. For (6+9)/6, correct is 15/6 = 5/2, not 9.
- **Quiz result:** `5/2`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 2. Matching addend trap (Class 6+)

- **Teach figure:** `(44+11)/11`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(36+12)/12`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel the two 11s → 44+1 = 45 (wrong).
- **Why (quiz):** Same trap as the card. For (36+12)/12, correct is 48/12 = 4, not 37.
- **Quiz result:** `4`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 3. Same idea with subtraction (Class 6+)

- **Teach figure:** `(15-5)/5`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(21-7)/7`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel both 5s → 14 or 1 (both wrong).
- **Why (quiz):** Same rule as the card. For (21−7)/7, correct is 14/7 = 2, not 20.
- **Quiz result:** `2`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 4. Cut must hit every term (Class 6+)

- **Teach figure:** `(12+8)/4`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(18+12)/6`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel only 12 with 4 → 11 (wrong).
- **Why (quiz):** Same rule as the card. For (18+12)/6, every term must be divided: 3+2 = 5, not 15.
- **Quiz result:** `5`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 5. Three terms (Class 6–7)

- **Teach figure:** `(6+9+3)/3`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(8+12+4)/4`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel the last 3 → 15 (wrong).
- **Why (quiz):** Same rule as the card. For (8+12+4)/4, split all three: 2+3+1 = 6.
- **Quiz result:** `6`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 6. When cutting is allowed (Class 6–7)

- **Teach figure:** `(2×7+2)/2`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(3×5+3)/3`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cut only one lone 2 → claim 14 (did not take common factor).
- **Why (quiz):** Same as the card: take common first. For (3×5+3)/3, factor 3(5+1)/3 = 6. Cutting one lone 3 → 15 is wrong.
- **Quiz result:** `6`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (1), (3) and (4) are correct; (2) is wrong`

## Card 7. Algebra — split (Class 7–8)

- **Teach figure:** `(4x+2)/2`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(6x+3)/3`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel only the +2 → 4x+1 (wrong).
- **Why (quiz):** Same as the card. For (6x+3)/3, split or factor: answer is 2x+1, not 6x+1.
- **Quiz result:** `2x+1`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 8. Algebra — do not cut x in a sum (Class 7–8)

- **Teach figure:** `(4x+2)/x`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(6x+3)/x`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel x in 4x → claim 6 (classic trap).
- **Why (quiz):** Same as the card. For (6x+3)/x, correct is 6 + 3/x. Cutting x to claim 9 is wrong.
- **Quiz result:** `6 + 3/x`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (4) are correct; (1) and (3) are wrong`

## Card 9. Algebra — subtract then factor (Class 7–8)

- **Teach figure:** `(6x-3)/3`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(30x-5)/10`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cancel only −3 → 6x−1 (wrong).
- **Why (quiz):** Same as the card. For (30x−5)/10, split: 3x − 1/2, or factor: 5(6x−1)/10 = (6x−1)/2.
- **Quiz result:** `3x - 1/2`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 10. Master — factor then cut (Class 8+)

- **Teach figure:** `(2x+2)/(x+1)`
- **Quiz sum (different numbers):** `(3x+3)/(x+1)`
- **Wrong cancel (teach):** Cut x with x → claim 2+2 = 4 (classic trap).
- **Why (quiz):** Same as the card. For (3x+3)/(x+1), factor: 3(x+1)/(x+1) = 3 (x≠−1). Cutting x to claim 6 is wrong.
- **Quiz result:** `3`
- **MCQ:** `Steps (2) and (3) are correct; (1) and (4) are wrong`
