# Fraction Simplification Cards (Class 6+)

Common misunderstanding: students cancel a number/variable that is only **part of a sum or difference**.

**Golden rule:** You may cancel only a common **factor** of the *whole* numerator and the denominator.

If the numerator is a sum/difference, use either:

1. **Split** — `(a+b)/c = a/c + b/c` (when `c ≠ 0`)
2. **Factor then cancel** — only after a common factor appears on the whole top

Use these cards instead of rote times-tables drills when you want students to *see* the cancel trap.

## Card 1. Cancel trap — numbers in a sum (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(4+5)/4`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel both 4s → left with 5 (illegal).
- **Why / correct idea:** The bar means the whole numerator is divided by 4. So (4+5)/4 = 4/4 + 5/4 = 1 + 5/4 = 9/4, not 5.
- **Target result:** `9/4`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel 4s: get 5
  - (2) [TRUE] Add first: 9/4
  - (3) [TRUE] Split: 4/4 + 5/4 = 9/4
  - (4) [FALSE] Cancel one digit only: get 45/4
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 2. Same trap with a matching addend (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(44+11)/11`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel the two 11s → 44+1 = 45 (very common, still wrong).
- **Why / correct idea:** 11 divides the whole sum 44+11. Correct: 55/11 = 5, or 44/11 + 11/11 = 4+1 = 5. Never 45.
- **Target result:** `5`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel 11s: 44+1 = 45
  - (2) [TRUE] Add first: 55/11 = 5
  - (3) [TRUE] Split: 44/11 + 11/11 = 5
  - (4) [FALSE] Cancel part of 44: 4+11 = 15
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 3. Subtraction — same rule (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(15-5)/5`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel both 5s → 15−1 = 14, or just 1 (both wrong).
- **Why / correct idea:** Minus does not allow cancel of a single term. (15−5)/5 = 10/5 = 2, or 15/5 − 5/5 = 3−1 = 2.
- **Target result:** `2`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel 5s → 15−1 = 14
  - (2) [TRUE] Subtract first: 10/5 = 2
  - (3) [TRUE] Split: 15/5 − 5/5 = 2
  - (4) [FALSE] Cancel 5s → get 1
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 4. Denominator divides every term (Class 6+)

- **Hook:** `(12+8)/4`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel only 12 with 4 → 3+8 = 11 (wrong).
- **Why / correct idea:** 4 must divide both 12 and 8. 12/4 + 8/4 = 3+2 = 5, or (12+8)/4 = 20/4 = 5.
- **Target result:** `5`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel 12 with 4 → 3+8 = 11
  - (2) [TRUE] Add first: 20/4 = 5
  - (3) [TRUE] Split: 12/4 + 8/4 = 5
  - (4) [FALSE] Cancel 8 with 4 → 12+2 = 14
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 5. Three terms — cancel still illegal (Class 6–7)

- **Hook:** `(6+9+3)/3`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel the last 3 with the denominator → 6+9 = 15 (wrong).
- **Why / correct idea:** Every term is divided by 3: 6/3 + 9/3 + 3/3 = 2+3+1 = 6. Or add first: 18/3 = 6.
- **Target result:** `6`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel last 3 → 6+9 = 15
  - (2) [TRUE] Add first: 18/3 = 6
  - (3) [TRUE] Split all three: 2+3+1 = 6
  - (4) [FALSE] Random cancel → claim 5
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 6. Golden rule — when cancel IS allowed (Class 6–7)

- **Hook:** `2(7+1)/2 vs (2+7)/2`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Students mix factor-cancel with sum-cancel.
- **Why / correct idea:** Cancel only a common FACTOR of the whole top and bottom. 2(7+1)/2 → cancel factor 2 → 8 is legal. (2+7)/2 cannot cancel the 2; correct value is 9/2.
- **Target result:** `8 vs 9/2`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [TRUE] 2(7+1)/2 → cancel factor 2 → 8
  - (2) [FALSE] (2+7)/2 → cancel the 2 → 7
  - (3) [TRUE] (2+7)/2 = 2/2 + 7/2 = 9/2
  - (4) [TRUE] 2(7+1)/2 = 16/2 = 8
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (1), (3) and (4) are correct; (2) is wrong`

## Card 7. Algebra — split the fraction (Class 7–8)

- **Hook:** `(4x+2)/2`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel only the +2 with denominator 2 → 4x+1 (wrong).
- **Why / correct idea:** Split: 4x/2 + 2/2 = 2x + 1. Or factor: 2(2x+1)/2 = 2x+1. Cancelling only one term is illegal.
- **Target result:** `2x+1`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel the +2 → 4x+1
  - (2) [TRUE] Split: 4x/2 + 2/2 = 2x+1
  - (3) [TRUE] Factor: 2(2x+1)/2 = 2x+1
  - (4) [FALSE] Leave as 4x+2 (not simplified)
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 8. Algebra — never cancel a variable in a sum (Class 7–8)

- **Hook:** `(4x+2)/x`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel x in 4x with denominator x → 4+2 = 6 (classic trap).
- **Why / correct idea:** x is not a factor of the whole numerator. Correct: 4x/x + 2/x = 4 + 2/x. Not 6. Check x=2: left side is 5, so '6' fails.
- **Target result:** `4 + 2/x`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel x → 4+2 = 6
  - (2) [TRUE] Split: 4x/x + 2/x = 4 + 2/x
  - (3) [FALSE] Cancel x → get 4
  - (4) [TRUE] For x=2: value is 5, while '6' fails
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (4) are correct; (1) and (3) are wrong`

## Card 9. Algebra subtraction + factor path (Class 7–8)

- **Hook:** `(6x-3)/3`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Cancel only −3 with 3 → 6x−1 (wrong).
- **Why / correct idea:** Split: 6x/3 − 3/3 = 2x − 1. Or factor: 3(2x−1)/3 = 2x−1.
- **Target result:** `2x-1`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Cancel −3 → 6x−1
  - (2) [TRUE] Split: 2x − 1
  - (3) [TRUE] Factor: 3(2x−1)/3 = 2x−1
  - (4) [FALSE] Claim answer is 6x
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are both correct`

## Card 10. Master card — factor vs illegal cancel (Class 8+)

- **Hook:** `(2x+2)/(x+1)`
- **Wrong cancel students try:** Piece-cancel the +2, or cancel x terms carelessly.
- **Why / correct idea:** Factor numerator: 2(x+1)/(x+1) = 2 (for x ≠ −1). You may cancel the common factor (x+1), not random pieces of the sum. Cancelling x terms 'to get 2' is illegal method (fails at x=0).
- **Target result:** `2`
- **Check steps:**
  - (1) [FALSE] Piece-cancel → 2x/(x+1)
  - (2) [TRUE] Factor: 2(x+1)/(x+1) = 2
  - (3) [TRUE] For x=3: left = 8/4 = 2, matches
  - (4) [FALSE] Cancel x top/bottom (illegal method)
- **MCQ correct option:** `Steps (2) and (3) are correct; (1) is wrong; (4) method is illegal`
